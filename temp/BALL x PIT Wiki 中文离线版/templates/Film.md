#### 描述

要使用此模板， 请添加 {} {}电影模板并填入相应字段。 左空字段不会出现在文章中。 此信息框模板的使用 [方制信息箱语法](https://ballxpit.wiki.gg/wiki/W%3AHelp%3AInfobox?action=edit&redlink=1).

#### 语法

```
{{Film
 | title    =
 | image    =
 | caption  =
 | premiere =
 | genre    =
 | rating   =
 | runtime  =
 | director =
 | writer   =
 | music    =
 | producer =
 | budget   =
 | earned   =
 | previous =
 | next     =
}}
```
