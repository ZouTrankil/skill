#### 描述

要使用此模板， 请添加 {} {}[系列](Series.md)模板并填入相应字段。 左空字段不会出现在文章中。 此信息框模板的使用 [方制信息箱语法](https://ballxpit.wiki.gg/wiki/W%3AHelp%3AInfobox?action=edit&redlink=1).

#### 语法

```
{{Series
 | title     =
 | image     =
 | caption   =
 | release   =
 | seasons   =
 | episodes  =
 | runtime   =
 | genre     =
 | network   =
 | distrib   =
 | creator   =
 | writer    =
 | director  =
 | composer  =
 | based on  =
 | exec prod =
 | producer  =
 | prod co   =
 | country   =
 | language  =
}}
```
