## 说明

要使用此模板，请添加 {{[Film](Film.md)}} 模板并填写相应的字段。未填写的字段将不会出现在文章中。此信息框模板使用 [Fandom 的信息框语法](https://ballxpit.wiki.gg/wiki/W%3AHelp%3AInfobox?action=edit&redlink=1)。

## 语法

```
{{Film
 | title    =
 | image    =
 | caption  =
 | premiere =
 | genre    =
 | rating   =
 | runtime  =
 | director =
 | writer   =
 | music    =
 | producer =
 | budget   =
 | earned   =
 | previous =
 | next     =
}}
```
