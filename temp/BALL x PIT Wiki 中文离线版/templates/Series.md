## 说明

要使用此模板，请添加 {{系列}} 模板并填写相应的字段。未填写的字段将不会出现在文章中。此信息框模板使用 [Fandom 的信息框语法](https://ballxpit.wiki.gg/wiki/W%3AHelp%3AInfobox?action=edit&redlink=1)。

## 语法

```
{{Series
 | title     =
 | image     =
 | caption   =
 | release   =
 | seasons   =
 | episodes  =
 | runtime   =
 | genre     =
 | network   =
 | distrib   =
 | creator   =
 | writer    =
 | director  =
 | composer  =
 | based on  =
 | exec prod =
 | producer  =
 | prod co   =
 | country   =
 | language  =
}}
```
